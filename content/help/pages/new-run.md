**New run** (Wholesale → New run, or **New run** on [Runs](/help/pages/runs)) is where every screening run starts. Pick the source with the tabs at the top; each tab is a link (`/new-run?tab=…`), so you can bookmark the one you use most.

| Tab | What it screens | Article |
|---|---|---|
| **Upload file** | Supplier price lists, xlsx or csv, several at once | [Upload](/help/pages/upload) |
| **Check ASINs** | ASINs, EANs or Amazon links you paste, with an optional landed cost | [Check ASINs](/help/pages/check) |
| **Qogita** | In-stock products pulled from Qogita; saved pulls re-run nightly | [Qogita](/help/pages/qogita) |
| **Hunt** | Products your profile wants, found with Keepa's Product Finder | [Hunt](/help/pages/hunt) |
| **Scan a seller** | A seller's storefront | [Sellers](/help/pages/sellers) |

Whatever the source, the result is a run: it appears in [Runs](/help/pages/runs) and screens in the background.

The old addresses still work: `/upload`, `/check`, `/qogita`, `/hunt` and `/sellers/scan` open the matching tab here. ⌘K → **Check an ASIN** opens the Check ASINs tab with the ASIN you typed.
