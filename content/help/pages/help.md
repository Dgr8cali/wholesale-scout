The help centre explains every page, gate and number in the app. The articles are written from the code and kept in the repo, so they change when the app does.

## Search

Type in the box at the top of **Help**. It matches article titles, their text, and other words for the same thing: "ungate" finds [Apply for brand approval](/help/howto/apply-for-brand-approval), "tokens" finds [Keepa tokens](/help/concepts/keepa-tokens). Results that match in the title or those other words come first. Every word you type has to appear somewhere in the article, so use fewer words if nothing comes up.

With the box empty, the page lists every article by section:

| Section | What's in it |
|---|---|
| Start here | [Getting started](/help/getting-started): from a price list to an order |
| Pages | One article per page, every control on it |
| How it works | [Score](/help/concepts/score), [Fee engine](/help/concepts/fees), [Profiles](/help/concepts/profiles), [Keepa tokens](/help/concepts/keepa-tokens) |
| Gates | One article per gate: what it checks, its settings, its mode in each profile, how to read its why-line |
| How-tos | Step by step: change a threshold, waive a gate, apply for a brand, and more |
| Reference | The [Glossary](/help/reference/glossary) |

## The ? on every page

The **?** in the top bar opens the current page's article in a panel on the right, so you can read it next to what it describes. Links inside the panel open in the panel; the back arrow returns to the previous article. **Open as a page** shows the article full width. Close the panel with the ×, Escape, or a click outside it.

## Gate names in a row's details

In an expanded row (or the details drawer), each gate's name under **Gates** is a link: click it to open that gate's article in the panel. The article explains what the gate checks and how to read the line next to it.
