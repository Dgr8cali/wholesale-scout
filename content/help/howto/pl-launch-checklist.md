**Private label → Launch** lists the candidates ready to launch (verdict **Order samples**, or status samples or launched; **Show all candidates** for the rest) with their next step. Each such candidate's page also has a **Launch** section under Quotes.

## The checklist

In order:

1. Samples ordered
2. Samples received
3. Sample chosen
4. Trademark filed (UK IPO, £170 for one class)
5. PO placed
6. Pre-shipment inspection booked
7. Inspection passed
8. Shipped
9. At FBA
10. Listing live
11. Ads launched
12. First review

Each step has a **done** tick (today's date is filled in; change it), a **note**, and a **cost** in £. The next step is highlighted, and the candidates list shows it.

**The status follows**: anything done moves a draft to **researching**, samples ordered to **samples**, listing live to **launched**. It only moves forwards, and never changes a dropped candidate.

**Ads launched** links to **Ads → Launch** with the candidate's listing ASIN, its Gate 5 head terms and its page-one competitors filled in (see [Ads: launching a product](/help/howto/ads-launching-a-product)).

## Listing ASIN

Once your listing exists, type its ASIN. The candidate becomes an Ads product carrying its landed cost (the chosen quote's, else Gate 0's), so **profit after ads** shows on the candidate (with ad spend, sales, ACoS and break-even) and on the Ads dashboard, which links back to the candidate.

## Budget

Gate 7's lines (stock = units × landed, samples, inspection, photography, trademark, launch ads, and the 10% buffer) beside what the checklist says was spent on each. Each step's cost counts towards its line: samples (steps 1–2), trademark (4), stock (PO placed and shipped: the deposit, the balance, freight, duty and import VAT), inspection (6–7), photography (listing live), launch ads (ads launched), other (the rest). The bar shows the spend against your budget (Settings → Private label, £1,000), with a mark at Gate 7's planned total.
