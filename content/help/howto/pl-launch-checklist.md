Each candidate's page has a **Launch** tab: one flow in four steps. **1. Checklist** (below), **2. Inbound / stock check** (the listing ASIN's stock in each bucket, days of cover and its purchases, with the shipping steps ticked so far), **3. Ads launch plan** (the same builder as **Ads → Launch**, filled from the candidate) and **4. Export bulk file** (create the sheet and download it). The old Private label → Launch address opens the Launch tab of the candidate you're launching. The header's primary button ticks the next checklist step (for example **Mark samples received**), and the Pipeline moves the card to Launch and Live as the steps are ticked.

## The checklist

In order:

1. Samples ordered
2. Samples received
3. Sample chosen
4. Trademark filed (UK IPO, £170 for one class)
5. PO placed
6. Pre-shipment inspection booked
7. Inspection passed
8. Shipped
9. At FBA
10. Listing written (its link opens the candidate's **Listing** section: see [Private label: writing the listing](/help/howto/pl-writing-the-listing))
11. Listing live
12. Ads launched
13. First review

Each step has a **done** tick (today's date is filled in; change it), a **note**, and a **cost** in £. The next step is highlighted, and the candidates list shows it.

**The status follows**: anything done moves a draft to **researching**, samples ordered to **samples**, listing live to **launched**. It only moves forwards, and never changes a dropped candidate.

**Ads launched** links to step 3, the Ads launch plan, with the candidate's listing ASIN, its Gate 5 head terms and its page-one competitors filled in (see [Ads: launching a product](/help/howto/ads-launching-a-product)).

## Listing ASIN

Once your listing exists, type its ASIN. The candidate becomes an Ads product carrying its landed cost (the chosen quote's, else Gate 0's), so **profit after ads** shows on the candidate (with ad spend, sales, ACoS and break-even) and on the Ads dashboard, which links back to the candidate.

## Budget

Gate 7's lines (stock = units × landed, samples, inspection, photography, trademark, launch ads, and the 10% buffer) beside what the checklist says was spent on each. Each step's cost counts towards its line: samples (steps 1–2), trademark (4), stock (PO placed and shipped: the deposit, the balance, freight, duty and import VAT), inspection (6–7), photography (listing live), launch ads (ads launched), other (the rest). The bar shows the spend against your launch capital (the candidate's **Capital for this launch** in Gate 7, else **Launch capital** in Settings → Business, default £1,000), with a mark at Gate 7's planned total.
