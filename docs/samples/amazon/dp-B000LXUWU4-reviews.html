    “Good at tackling algae, but requires patience and good tank habits.”

    I’ve used Tetra AlguMin several times in my freshwater aquarium. It’s very easy to dose — just measure 5 ml per 10 litres — and it disperses quickly without clouding the water. Within a few days I noticed most green algae on the glass and decorations starting to die off, and after a week or so the tank looked noticeably cleaner.

    That said, it’s not a magic bullet. Black beard algae and some stubborn blue-green patches didn’t disappear completely after the first dose. I also had to manually remove dead algae and make sure my filter and aeration were running well, otherwise oxygen levels dipped a little.

    Overall, AlguMin is a good product to control light to moderate algae growth if you combine it with proper maintenance (water changes, balanced lighting and feeding). I’d recommend it as part of a broader algae-management plan rather than a one-time cure.
